Research

Deep learning for RNA structure in cryo-EM

RNA folds into recurring structural motifs such as hairpins, bulges, and internal loops. Recognizing them directly in medium- and low-resolution cryo-EM density maps can make RNA model building faster and more reliable.

View on Google Scholar Dataset & code

3DEM-RNA-Motif Dataset

An open benchmark of 125,000+ segmented cryo-EM density patches across 25 RNA motif types, with an open-source pipeline and CLI for training and classification.

Cryo-EMDatasetOpen source

Motif Detection & Structure Prediction

3D CNN motif classifiers, FFT-based template matching for localisation, and a UNETR vision-transformer backbone segmentation model validated on experimental maps.

Blind test: macro sensitivity 0.836 · specificity 0.958

3D CNNUNETRMONAI

Survey: DL for RNA 3D Structure

A systematic review of deep learning methods for reconstructing RNA tertiary structure from cryo-EM maps.
